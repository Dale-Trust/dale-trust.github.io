## Who can travel

Dale Trust coaches are open to **all Rochdale AFC supporters** — you do not need to be a Trust member to book.

Trust members get **priority booking**: seats open to members first, and to everyone else **[how long after? e.g. 5 days before departure, or once seats are running short]**.

The Trust runs the official supporters' coach, and it is the only coach promoted by Rochdale AFC.

## Booking and payment

- Seats are booked through daletrust.uk and **paid for at the time of booking**
- A booking is only confirmed once payment is received
- Prices are per seat and shown on each fixture — currently **£35** for longer trips such as Newport and Colchester
- **[Do you want a different price for members, juniors or concessions, or one price for everyone?]**

## Departures

- Coaches leave from the **Crown Oil Arena** unless stated otherwise
- Departure times are shown when you book, and are usually **[how long?]** before kick-off
- **Please arrive ten minutes before departure.** The coach leaves on time and cannot wait for latecomers
- Most journeys include a stop each way
- **[Are there any other pick-up points, or is it Crown Oil Arena only?]**

## Refunds and cancellations

- **If the coach is cancelled, or the match is postponed, everyone gets a full refund.** No exceptions, no quibbling — if it is out of your control, you get your money back
- **If you don't turn up, there is no refund.** The coach is paid for whether the seat is filled or not. Please tell us as early as you can if you can't travel — the earlier we know, the better the chance of reselling the seat
- **[If a passenger cancels their own seat in advance — what happens? Suggested: full refund up to 48 hours before departure; after that we will refund if the seat can be resold, since the coach is paid for either way]**
- Refunds are returned by the same method used to pay

## On the coach

- The coach must be treated with respect. Anyone causing damage will be liable for the cost
- The driver's instructions must be followed at all times
- Please take your litter off with you

### Alcohol

It is against the law to carry alcohol on a vehicle travelling to a designated football match, and both the operator and passengers can be prosecuted. **Alcohol is not permitted on Trust coaches.**

### Behaviour

The Trust reserves the right to refuse travel to anyone whose behaviour puts other passengers, the driver or the Trust's reputation at risk. In the unlikely event that happens, no refund will be given.

## Young supporters

- **[Under what age must a young supporter be accompanied by an adult? Common practice is under 16, but it's your call]**
- An accompanying adult is responsible for the young person for the whole journey

## Match tickets

- **Your coach seat does not include a match ticket.** Please buy your ticket separately from the club
- Some away clubs will not sell tickets on the day, so book early

## Your details

We collect your name, contact number and email so we can run the coach and reach you if plans change — for example a change of departure time, or a postponement.

- Details are held securely and used only for coach travel
- They are not passed to anyone else
- If you are not a Trust member, booking a coach does not make you one and does not add you to our mailing list

Our full [privacy policy](privacy.html) explains how we handle personal information.
