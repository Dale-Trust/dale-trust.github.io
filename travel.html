Before you book

  • Your coach seat does not include a match ticket — please buy that from the club
  • Please arrive ten minutes before departure
  • All passengers aged 15 or under MUST travel with a responsible adult aged 18 or over
  • No Alcohol / Vaping on board
  • No refund for no-shows. The coach is paid for whether you're on it or not, so please let us know as early as you can if you can't travel
  • Any questions: travel@daletrust.co.uk
