How to do it. Open your spreadsheet, select the rows you want — not the headings — copy them, and paste into the box above. One line per person.

What it looks for. A number at the start is the position. The next column is the name. Anything that is a word becomes the small grey line underneath — the manager, or Adult and Exile. The first number after the name is the points shown on the right.

So a line like 1  Locomotiv Moocow  Roger Winterbottom  Exile  176 becomes 1 · Locomotiv Moocow · Roger Winterbottom · Exile · 176.
