Notice of Annual General Meeting

Rochdale Supporters Club Limited (Dale Supporters Trust)

Dear member,

Notice is hereby given that the Annual General Meeting of the Society will be held at:

Date: Saturday 28 November 2026
Time: 11am (doors open 10:30am)
Place: The Dale Bar, Rochdale AFC

Every member is welcome to attend, speak and, if aged 16 or over, vote.

Business of the meeting

  1. Welcome, introductions and apologies
  2. Minutes of the 2025 AGM
  3. Treasurer’s report and accountant’s statement
  4. To allow acceptance of the unaudited accounts for the 12 months period ending 30th June 2026
  5. To receive and adopt the unaudited accounts for the 12 months period ending 30th June 2026
  6. To disapply the requirement for an audit of the Society’s accounts for the year ending 30th June 2027
  7. To confirm the appointment of the Society’s accountants, Horsfield and Smith
  8. Resignation of Board members
  9. Directors not seeking re-election
  10. Election of Directors for the year ending 30th June 2027: Emma Loynd, Michael Shepherd, Vince Ashall, Keith Hicks, Siobhan McElhinney, Scott Goulding, Ian Goodwin and Chris Culshaw
  11. Appointment of volunteers
  12. Any other business and questions to the Board

The full agenda and the minutes of the 2025 AGM are in the AGM area of the members’ website. The accounts will be available online in the AGM area, and a signed copy will be available once they have been signed.

Can’t make it? Vote online

If you are 16 or over you can appoint the Chair of the meeting as your proxy and tell them how to vote on each item, without leaving your sofa.

  • Online voting opens on Wednesday 18 November and closes at 5pm on Tuesday 24 November.
  • Go to the AGM page and ask for a sign-in link to the email address we hold for you. There is no password.
  • You can change or withdraw your instruction at any time before it closes. If you come to the meeting and vote in person, that replaces your online instruction.
  • Prefer paper? The proxy form is attached to this email. Post it to Dale Supporters Trust, PO Box 1907, Rochdale, OL16 9EX, or scan it and email it to info@daletrust.uk, to arrive by 5pm on Tuesday 24 November.

Planning to come?

Tell us on the AGM page so we can plan the room. It is optional and not binding.

Junior members are very welcome at the meeting, but under the Society’s rules only members aged 16 or over may vote.

If you have any problem signing in, or think we hold the wrong email address for you, just reply to this email.
