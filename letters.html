The wording

One letter that changes itself depending on who it is going to. Edit it here and every letter follows.

{{first_name}}{{member_no}}{{season}}{{donation}}{{type}}
{{#junior}}…{{/junior}}{{#new}}…{{/new}}{{#renewal}}…{{/renewal}}{{#donation}}…{{/donation}}

Anything between {{#junior}} and {{/junior}} only appears on junior letters. Same for new, renewal and donation. {{^junior}} means everyone except juniors. A line starting with # becomes a heading, and **bold** comes out bold.
