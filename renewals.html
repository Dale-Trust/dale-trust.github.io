When this is open, members signing in see a renew button for next season, and the reminder emails can go out.

Rolling the season over

Once the new season has started and renewals are largely done, roll it over. Next season becomes this season, the membership figures for the season just gone are recorded permanently, and renewals close.

Do this once a year, after the season has changed. Nothing is deleted — every member record stays exactly where it is.
