What is this for

Notices, not news.

An AGM notice, coach seats left with a deadline, something for exiles only, a members’ survey. Anything that is genuinely news should be written as an article instead — that way it lives on the website and people can find it later.

This is not published anywhere. It goes to inboxes only, and there is no link back to the site. A record of what was sent and to whom is kept below.
