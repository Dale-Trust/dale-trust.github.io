Trust email addresses

Who has what, and where the shared ones go — tap any address to copy it

Everyone

Each person has a personal address and a role address. Both arrive in the same inbox.

Loading…
Reply from the role address when it is Trust business. Both come to the same place, but there is a “From” dropdown when you write an email. That way anything on the website or a poster keeps working when someone else takes the job on.

Shared addresses

More than one person receives these, and anyone on them can reply as the address rather than as themselves.

info@, help@ and support@ all reach the same people. Three addresses because a member who is stuck at eleven at night will type whichever comes to mind. They cost nothing.

Getting it on your phone

Nothing to set up beyond signing in.

On your phone — download Zoho Mail from the App Store or Play Store and sign in.

On a computer — go to mail.zoho.eu and sign in there. Nothing to install.

Getting it into your normal Mail app or Outlook is coming — for now this gets you going.
