Everyone

Reply from the role address when it is Trust business. Both come to the same place, but there is a “From” dropdown when you write an email. That way anything on the website or a poster keeps working when someone else takes the job on.

Shared addresses

More than one person receives these, and anyone on them can reply as the address rather than as themselves.

info@, help@ and support@ all reach the same people. Three addresses because a member who is stuck at eleven at night will type whichever comes to mind. They cost nothing.

Getting your email on your phone

Two ways. The first takes a minute; the second puts it in the app you already use.

The quick way — the Zoho app

Download Zoho Mail from the App Store or Play Store and sign in with your Trust address and password. That is it.

On a computer, go to mail.zoho.eu and sign in there. Nothing to install.

The better way — your own Mail app

Your Trust email then sits alongside everything else, and you are not opening another app to check it.

iPhone or iPad
  1. Settings → Apps → Mail → Mail Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Mail Account
  2. Put in your name, your Trust address and your password, then Next
  3. Choose IMAP at the top
  4. Incoming — Host imap.zoho.eu, username your full email address, and your password
  5. Outgoing — Host smtp.zoho.eu, same username and password
  6. Save
Android
  1. Open Gmail → tap your picture, top right → Add another account → Other
  2. Put your Trust address in, tap Next, then choose Personal (IMAP)
  3. Password, then Next
  4. Incoming server imap.zoho.eu, Outgoing smtp.zoho.eu
  5. Finish
Outlook, on a computer
  1. File → Add Account
  2. Put your Trust address in and choose Advanced options → Let me set up my account manually
  3. Choose IMAP
  4. Incoming imap.zoho.eu, port 993, SSL
  5. Outgoing smtp.zoho.eu, port 465, SSL
  6. Password, then Connect
If it will not connect, it is almost always one of two things. Check the server is .eu and not .com — the Trust is on the European servers. And your username is your whole email address, not just the bit before the @.
Once it is set up, send yourself a test. Then reply to it choosing your role address from the “From” dropdown, so you know where to find it when it matters.
