Shopping that pays the Trust back

Shop as normal. The Trust gets paid.

Over 8,000 retailers will donate a slice of whatever you spend to the Dale Trust — Amazon, Argos, eBay, Sainsbury’s, Trainline, the lot. It costs you nothing. The retailer pays it, not you.

Sign up once, add the reminder to your browser, and it happens by itself every time you shop.
