Andy's Man Club

Men's mental health · Rochdale

Andy's Man Club runs free peer-to-peer support groups for men aged 18 and over, meeting every Monday evening (except bank holidays) at 7pm. There's no referral, no waiting list and no cost — you just turn up.

Their message is a simple one, and one football grounds are a good place to spread: #ITSOKAYTOTALK.

Find your nearest group andysmanclub.co.uk

Rochdale DS All Stars

Football for children with Down syndrome

Rochdale DS All Stars give children and young people with Down syndrome the chance to play football in a supportive, properly coached environment — building confidence and friendships alongside the skills.

The Trust supports the All Stars through fundraising and appeals to its members. Every pound raised goes directly to keeping the sessions running.

Her Game Too

Tackling sexism in football

Her Game Too is a national campaign challenging sexism in football and making grounds welcoming places for women and girls — as supporters, players, staff and officials.

The Trust stands behind the campaign at the Crown Oil Arena, and works with the club to make sure every Dale supporter feels equally at home in the stands.
